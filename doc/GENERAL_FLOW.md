# ### E-Commerce General Flow (Non-Technical Overview)

This document explains, in simple terms, how the platform works for customers, vendors, riders, and administrators within the campus and surrounding community ecosystem.

---

## Customer Flow

- **Guest access**: Browse available vendors, view products/services, and explore categories (food, fashion, electronics, services, etc.). To place orders, you must log in.

- **Register and login**: Create an account using phone number or email. Login is simple and secure. You can also start browsing first and register later during checkout.

- **Verify OTP and activate account**: After registering, you receive a one-time code via SMS or email to confirm your account and enable full access.

- **Profile and settings**: Manage your personal details, delivery preferences, and saved locations (hostel, home, campus spots).

- **Receive alerts and notifications**: Get updates about order status, vendor responses, promotions, and important announcements via in-app notifications, SMS, or email.

- **Set and manage delivery locations**: Save multiple locations (e.g., hostel, lecture halls, nearby residences) and choose a default for faster ordering.

- **Search and discover vendors/products**: Easily find vendors or items using search. Explore by categories such as food, groceries, clothing, electronics, and services offered by community vendors.

- **Filter and sort**: Narrow results by price, popularity, vendor rating, availability, and distance.

- **Vendor profiles**: View vendor details including available products, pricing, ratings, and reviews.

- **Product details**: See item descriptions, pricing, availability, and options (e.g., size, quantity, customization if applicable).

- **Cart management**: Add items from one or multiple vendors to your cart. Adjust quantities, remove items, and review totals before checkout.

- **Apply offers or discounts**: Use promo codes or vendor-specific offers where available.

- **Checkout**: Confirm delivery location, select delivery or pickup, and choose your payment method.

- **Order options (flexibility)**:
  - Immediate order: Place and pay instantly
  - Scheduled order: Choose a preferred delivery time
  - In-shop orders: Add to bill and pay later

- **Payments**:
  - M-Pesa
  - Card payments
  - Cash (on delivery or pickup)

- **Order tracking**:
  Placed → Accepted → Preparing → Ready → Out for Delivery → Delivered

- **Delivery or pickup**:
  - Rider delivery
  - Self pick-up

- **Receipts and history**: Every order generates a receipt. Customers can view past orders and reorder easily.

- **Reviews and ratings**: After receiving orders, customers can rate vendors and leave feedback.

---

## Vendor Flow (Business Owners)

- **Branch structure**: Vendors can operate with one or multiple branches. If a vendor has only one branch, it automatically serves as the main branch. If multiple branches exist, one is designated as the main branch while others operate as sub-branches under it.

- **Register as a vendor**: Sign up and create a business profile with name, category, and contact details.

- **Profile setup**: Add location, operating hours, and product/service listings.

- **Product/service management**: Upload items, set pricing, and update availability.

- **Order management**:
  - Receive orders in real time
  - Accept or reject requests
  - Update order status (Preparing → Ready)

- **Inventory management**: Track stock or service availability.

- **Payments handling**:
  - Receive payments through the platform
  - Transactions are tracked for transparency

- **Earnings tracking**: Monitor daily, weekly, and monthly performance.

- **Customer communication**: Respond to customer inquiries and special requests.

- **Promotions and offers**: Create discounts to attract more customers.

- **Reviews and reputation**: Build trust through ratings and feedback.

---

## Rider Flow (Delivery Personnel)

- **Register/login as rider**: Create an account and get verified.

- **View available deliveries**: See nearby delivery requests.

- **Accept delivery jobs**: Choose orders to deliver.

- **Pickup process**: Collect orders from vendors.

- **Delivery tracking**: Navigate to customer location and update status.

- **Complete delivery**: Mark orders as delivered.

- **Earnings tracking**: View completed deliveries and earnings.

---

## Admin & Super Admin Flow

- **Secure login**: Access admin dashboard with role-based permissions.

- **User management**:
  - Manage customers, vendors, and riders
  - Approve or suspend accounts

- **Vendor management**:
  - Verify vendors
  - Monitor performance

- **Order monitoring**:
  - Track all orders
  - Handle disputes

- **Payment oversight**:
  - Monitor transactions
  - Ensure correct distribution of funds

- **Commission and revenue control**:
  - Define platform earnings (fees, commissions)

- **Analytics and reporting**:
  - Track sales, users, and growth

- **Delivery system management**:
  - Monitor rider performance

- **Promotions and campaigns**:
  - Launch campaigns to increase engagement

- **Notifications and announcements**:
  - Send updates to users

- **Customer support system**:
  - Handle complaints and inquiries

---

## Overall Experience Summary

This platform connects customers, vendors, and riders into one ecosystem:

- Customers can easily discover and order from local vendors  
- Vendors can grow their businesses and manage operations efficiently  
- Riders earn through deliveries  
- Admins ensure smooth and fair platform operations  

The system improves:
- Convenience  
- Transparency  
- Economic opportunities within the community
